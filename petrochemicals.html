Research Themes

Key Investment & Risk Themes

Our analysts track the structural forces — technological, regulatory, and geopolitical — that are repricing assets across the petrochemical complex.

Feedstock Advantage Erosion

Middle East ethane subsidies under fiscal pressure; how Gulf crackers maintain cost leadership as global energy prices normalise.

China Overcapacity Spillover

PRC polymer and downstream surplus reshaping trade flows into Africa and ASEAN — identifying exposed producers and import-dependent markets.

Plastics Regulation Wave

Extended producer responsibility laws and single-use plastic bans across 40+ EM jurisdictions and their impact on demand forecasts.

Green & Blue Hydrogen Integration

Ammonia and methanol plants pivoting to low-carbon feedstocks — project finance structures, offtake risks, and first-mover economics.

Refinery-to-Chemicals Conversion

Crude-to-chemicals mega-complexes in Saudi Arabia, India, and Nigeria — capital costs, margin expectations, and integration value.

Sanctions & Trade Disruption

Iran and Russia petrochemical export redirection, grey-market flows, and the secondary sanction exposure for downstream buyers.
